Why This Programme Matters

The Reality

  • Malaysia’s technology sector is growing faster than the local talent pipeline can fill — creating a structural shortage of skilled graduates across software, cybersecurity, AI, and engineering
  • 59% of technology employers in Malaysia are actively expanding their headcount, and the competition for qualified graduates is intensifying every year
  • A strong foundation in mathematics, computing, and engineering thinking is the single most important determinant of success in any technology degree — and most students who struggle at degree level do so because their foundation was weak
  • Starting with the right pre-university programme is not just about meeting entry requirements — it is about arriving at your degree confident, capable, and ready to perform from day one

The Education

This is not a generic pre-university programme with computing modules added on. Every subject in the Foundation in Computing & Engineering is chosen because it directly prepares you for the demands of a technology or engineering degree. Mathematics, physics, programming, digital technology, and critical thinking are taught by experienced technology professionals, not just academics — giving you a first-year experience that already feels like working in the industry.

No prior coding experience is required. This programme is designed for students starting from scratch, and by the end of it you will have working knowledge of programming, digital systems, electric circuits, calculus, and statistics — the complete foundation for any computing or engineering degree.

The AI-Native Learning Experience

UNIMY is Malaysia's AI-native university. That identity starts here, from the very first semester of the Foundation programme. Students are introduced to digital technology, computing fundamentals, and programming in an environment where AI tools are part of how learning happens - not a topic saved for later years.

  • Industry-Grade Labs from Day One

    Train in computer labs and smart classrooms equipped with the latest software, simulation environments, and cybersecurity tools. You learn like a tech professional from your first week.

  • Taught by Real Tech Professionals

    Lecturers are experienced practitioners from computing, AI, electronics, and engineering industries. They bring real-world problems into the classroom and give you the professional context that textbooks cannot.

  • Coursera for Campus Access

    Foundation students have access to Coursera for Campus, enabling them to begin earning microcredentials from Google, IBM, AWS, and Microsoft alongside their foundation studies — building an industry-recognised profile before they even start their degree.

Entry Requirements

To be eligible for the Foundation in Computing & Engineering, applicants must meet one of the following:

  • Pass SPM / SPMV — credit in any 5 subjects
  • Pass IGCSE / O-Level — min Grade C in any 5 subjects
  • Pass UEC — min Grade B in any 3 subjects
  • Any equivalent qualification recognised by the Government of Malaysia

Trial examination and forecast results are accepted for early application.

Assessment Breakdown

  • 50% Coursework — assignments, quizzes, projects & presentations
  • 50% Final Examinations (certain modules assessed 100% by coursework)
